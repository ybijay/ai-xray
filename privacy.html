AI X-Ray privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

AI X-Ray is a Windows app, published on the Microsoft Store by Bijay Yerra, an independent developer. It shows what AI models running in Ollama are doing on your computer.

The short version

AI X-Ray doesn't collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. There's no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What AI X-Ray reads on your computer

To show it to you, the app reads:

  • your graphics card's name, memory, load and, on NVIDIA cards, temperature and power
  • your processor's name and your memory (RAM) use
  • your Windows version and computer name
  • the names of programs that are using graphics memory

This information is shown in the app's window and isn't sent anywhere.

The connections AI X-Ray makes

  • To Ollama. AI X-Ray talks to Ollama on your computer, or on another computer if you've pointed Ollama's OLLAMA_HOST setting at one. The questions you type in "X-ray a prompt" and the speed test go to that Ollama, which runs the model and sends the answer back. You control that Ollama; we never see what you type.
  • To model libraries, only when you look up a model. When you check a model in "Get a model", AI X-Ray reads that model's details from the Ollama library (registry.ollama.ai), from Hugging Face (hf.co), or from another model library if you type its address as part of the model name, just as Ollama itself would. Like any website visit, that service sees your internet address and the model's name, and its own privacy policy applies. The download itself is done by Ollama.
  • To the Microsoft Store, about your licence. The Store version asks Windows' Microsoft Store service whether your copy is in its free trial or has been bought. When you buy it, the purchase happens in the Store's own window and is handled by Microsoft; AI X-Ray never sees your payment details. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the Store.

AI X-Ray makes no other connections.

What AI X-Ray keeps on your computer

The app saves a few things in its own folder on your PC so they're there next time: your zoom level, your speed-test results, and context sizes it has learned for your models. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version deletes them.

AI X-Ray draws its window with Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows. Any diagnostic data WebView2 sends to Microsoft follows your Windows settings.

Children

AI X-Ray isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
